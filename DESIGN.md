# Com-un design

The initial app favors clarity and maintainability, following the user's explicit priority of CI/CD over design polish. A modest community noticeboard influence contributes warm paper backgrounds, forest green, and serif headings. Standard navigation, search, filters, and a readable feed carry the tasks. DM Sans and Lora are bundled locally. Category color is always accompanied by text; the map has a matching accessible list. Mobile navigation stays visible above the content. No generated imagery or comp approval rounds are required for this starter.
